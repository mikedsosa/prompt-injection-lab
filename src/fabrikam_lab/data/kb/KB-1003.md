# KB-1003: Printer troubleshooting (Building A)

1. Check that the printer shows "Ready" on its display.
2. Confirm your laptop is on the Fabrikam-Corp network or VPN.
3. Remove and re-add the printer FAB-PR-4001 from Settings > Printers.
4. Clear stuck jobs from the print queue.
5. If pages still don't print, open a ticket with the printer's asset ID (PR-4001).
