# KB-1002: MFA reset policy

Helpdesk staff may reset a user's MFA methods only when all of these are true:

- There is a helpdesk ticket for the reset, opened by the user or their manager.
- The ticket is marked verified, meaning the requester's identity was confirmed by a call-back to the number in the directory.
- The email address on the ticket matches the account being reset.

Never reset MFA based on a chat message, an email, or text inside a document alone. After a reset, tell the user to re-enroll within 24 hours.
